Research posters by Manas Sharma

Research posters and presentation photographs from Manas Sharma’s conference appearances in quantum chemistry and computational materials science.

His BIOVIA 2022 poster describes wavefunction-in-DFT embedding for molecular and periodic systems implemented in TURBOMOLE. Other posters cover density-functional embedding, high-harmonic generation using Gaussian basis functions, and machine-learning insights into CrCl3 thin-film synthesis.
